## Usage methodology

2393 metric series requested, 1654 with datapoints in 90 days. Daily period 86400 seconds; sums for count metrics, maximum for concurrency/age, mean of returned daily averages for latency/duration. **Latency averages here are not request-weighted percentiles or overall request-weighted means.** Missing datapoints are preserved; no filling with zero. A zero total with returned points means measured zero only in those sampled points. Missing metrics do not establish inactivity. [AWS ListMetrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/APIReference/API_ListMetrics.html) excludes series inactive for two weeks; direct resource-dimension queries supplement the catalogue for Lambda, DynamoDB, APIs, logs and S3. Older undiscovered dimensions, especially Cognito/EventBridge, remain a coverage limit.

Resource inventory includes mapped per-resource 30/90-day metrics, table capacity/throttles and S3 object/size observations. Nonstandard dimension series remain in raw evidence. No service was invoked for testing.
